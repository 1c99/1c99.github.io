DeepCatch M

End-to-End MLOps Pipeline for MRI Segmentation

Medical IP · Seoul, South Korea Nov 2023 – Present

Overview

DeepCatch M is an end-to-end MLOps pipeline for MRI segmentation covering the full ML lifecycle — data versioning, GPU training, experiment tracking, hyperparameter search, and containerized serving. Applied internally to SAT (subcutaneous adipose tissue) and VAT (visceral adipose tissue) segmentation at Medical IP. The public template uses BraTS brain-tumour data as a shareable demonstration of the same pipeline.
