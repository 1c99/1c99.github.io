Research Innovation

Conditional diffusion model extracts 2D aorta projections from X-rays. 3D volumetric extension via Latent Diffusion with 3D VAE, Flash Attention, and window attention for local context.

ConcatConditionedUNet with noise prediction (MSE loss). 4-GPU DDP training (NCCL), gradient accumulation (batch 512), mixed precision (FP16 + GradScaler), cosine LR with warmup.

Diffusion Training Results — X-ray → True Aorta → Generated Aorta (Dice 0.97)

Large-Scale Processing
