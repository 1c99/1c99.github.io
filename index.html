DeepCatch X (X-ray)

  • International expansion: reduced the inference pipeline's memory footprint enough to run the full portable X-ray bundle on a single laptop, supporting DK Medical (a major Korean X-ray manufacturer) in its Southeast Asia expansion (contracted ~500 systems in 2026 → ~2,000 in 2027).
  • Aorta: built the product's generative anatomy-extraction stack — a diffusion-based model for aorta extraction (Dice = 0.97, surpassing the GAN baseline), with downstream aortic-dilation detection reaching sensitivity 0.93 vs 0.31 for radiologists (Eur. J. Radiology, 2025).
  • Osteoporosis screening from chest radiographs: a single-network conditional rectified-flow model (x-prediction, shifted logit-normal timestep sampling, self-generated warm-start prior, RAD-DINO cross-attention with LoRA adaptation) that jointly predicts T12–L2 and clavicle masks, CT attenuation maps and bone-marrow HU, with a density-anchored readout that trains the mean-HU error directly in the flow objective.

  • Systems engineering: engineered a Ray-distributed inference pipeline (14-module DAG of actors) with a VLM-driven PA-view pre-filter; achieved 100x daily throughput and 2.8x per-case speedup via adaptive scheduling, shared memory, and TensorRT / ONNX / FP16 optimization. Applied to 2.3M+ cases.
  • Production & deployment: built a CI/CD-driven on-premise deployment pipeline for collaborative research with hospitals — reduced production codebase by 76% in LoC and compiled into source-encrypted standalone executables.
  • Data & regulatory: led end-to-end acquisition of 100TB+ medical datasets through multiple data vendors and built a multi-modal PHI de-identification pipeline (OCR+NER pixel masking, LLM metadata anonymization, 98% accuracy) for compliant data handling — underpinning FDA and MFDS approvals across the product line.
  • Technical presentation: presented DeepCatch X at NVIDIA GTC 2025 (exhibitor), demonstrating diffusion-based organ extraction and clinical quantification from CXR.

Diffusion Policies for Manipulation (simulation)
Built a vision + language-conditioned diffusion policy (frozen MiniLM + FiLM-conditioned DDPM) for 4-object pick-and-place: places the named block at 96% with zero wrong placements; ablations show causal grounding — it collapses without the instruction and obeys a swapped one (99% at 4-way, chance 25%). Compared imitation vs. RL on place-and-release: action-chunked behavior cloning 90% vs. offline RL (IQL) 98% (100 held-out episodes), with IQL's edge coming from failure data (70% on expert-only demos).
2026 | Side project
